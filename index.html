Conservation & Community Impact

TRAVEL THAT SUPPORTS TANZANIA

Run Africa is committed to travel as a direct catalyst for positive ecological stewardship. We believe genuine ecotourism protects fragile ecosystems only when the people who inhabit them share fairly in its prosperity.

forest

Habitat Preservation: A proportion of tour revenues is dedicated to park conservation fees and local anti-poaching initiatives.
